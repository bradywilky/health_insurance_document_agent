BINGLE-DINGLE INSURANCE — AUTHORIZATION AND INTAKE RULES
Document ID: BD-AUTH-2026. Version 1.0. Effective January 1–December 31, 2026.

1. Authorization scope
Under Meadow, DEMO-SESSION outpatient physical therapy requires a service-date authorization matching member, rendering provider, service category, date range, and authorized units. DEMO-VISIT office evaluation and DEMO-REMOTE virtual office visits do not require authorization under this rule. No rule for unlisted services or other products can be inferred.
Authorization does not prove active enrollment, remaining benefits, network status, clinical necessity, or payment eligibility. An authorization for one member or provider must not be reused for another.

2. Required claim intake information
Identify claim and line IDs; original or corrected submission; member ID and product; billing and rendering provider IDs; service date; submission receipt date; place of service; service code and modifiers if applicable; diagnosis information; units; billed amount and currency; and authorization reference when applicable. Link a corrected claim to its original. Unknown data must be labeled missing rather than silently defaulted.
Code definitions and clinical documentation must be verified from the applicable source records; do not infer a diagnosis or procedure from an undefined code.

3. Reviews
Check duplicate claim or line identifiers and overlapping original/corrected submissions before summing amounts. Missing or mismatched authorization is a review issue; this research tool must identify the missing or conflicting evidence, not automatically deny a claim. Refer to BD-AGR-2026-01 for submission timing and BD-BEN-MEADOW-2026 for benefit terms.
